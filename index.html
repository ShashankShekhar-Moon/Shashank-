The complete manual for Snake, Tetris and Sudoku. Open this page from the three-dot menu whenever you need the controls, rules or game-specific options.

01 / SNAKE

Eat the glowing food, grow longer and avoid biting your own body. The edges wrap around.

CONTROLS

Desktop: Arrow keys or W A S D. Phone/tablet: use the four direction controls or swipe on the board.

SETTINGS

Speed, music and restart are inside Settings. Pause/Resume stays directly in the game header.

02 / TETRIS

Move and rotate falling blocks and complete full horizontal rows.

CONTROLS

← → move · ↑ rotate · ↓ soft drop · DROP for an instant drop.

SETTINGS

Easy, Normal, Fast, Hard or Insane, music and restart are inside Settings.

03 / SUDOKU

Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with 1–9 exactly once.

CONTROLS

Tap a square and enter a number. Desktop users can use the keyboard.

SETTINGS

Difficulty, music, Check, Show Solution, New Puzzle and restart are inside Settings.
